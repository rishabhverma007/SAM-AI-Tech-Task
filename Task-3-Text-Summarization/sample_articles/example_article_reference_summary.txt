Artificial intelligence is transforming healthcare by helping radiologists read scans, enabling early disease detection, and predicting patient deterioration in hospitals. It also accelerates drug discovery by screening chemical compounds much faster than traditional methods. However, adoption faces serious challenges including data privacy, algorithmic bias, and regulatory approval, and doctors need explainable systems they can trust. With responsible collaboration between technologists, clinicians, and policymakers, AI could become one of the greatest medical breakthroughs of the century.
